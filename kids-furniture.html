FAQ

Kids furniture questions

What furniture does a preschool or daycare need?

Child-height tables and chairs for each age group, low open shelving and storage children can reach, a reading corner, role-play furniture such as a play kitchen, and soft seating. We plan these as themed learning areas: reading, blocks, art, making and role play.

What material is your kids furniture made from?

Premium SE0-grade birch plywood with extremely low formaldehyde emission, designed and made in-house in Korea.

Is the kids furniture safety certified?

Yes. The furniture holds Korea's KC safety certification for children's products and is tested under Korea's Special Act on the Safety of Children's Products.

Can you furnish a clubhouse kids room or show flat?

Yes. We can supply furniture alone or design the full kids room, combining furniture with an indoor play structure, flooring and wall graphics.
