Why indoor play matters in Indian cities

Summer heat, monsoon rain and winter air quality limit outdoor play for months each year. A well-designed indoor play area gives children a safe place to climb, slide and run every day of the year, and gives your building a reason for families to visit.

FAQ

Indoor play area questions

What is an indoor play area?

An indoor play area is a climate-controlled play space inside a building, such as a mall, clubhouse, hotel, preschool or play café, fitted with play frames, slides, climbing nets and soft play on safe flooring. It lets children play year-round regardless of heat, rain or pollution.

How much floor area does a kids indoor play area need?

A clubhouse kids room can work in a few hundred square feet; a mall play zone or play café usually needs a few thousand. Ceiling height matters as much as floor area, because double-height spaces allow multi-level net towers. We plan to your floor plate.

Do you design the full interior or just supply equipment?

Both. We design complete children's interiors, including play structures, themed walls, flooring, lighting and furniture, or we can supply play equipment into a space designed by your architect.

Is indoor play equipment safe for toddlers?

Yes, when toddlers have their own zone. We separate under-5 soft play from taller structures and use padded edges, enclosed platforms and impact-absorbing flooring. The play products carry Korean KC safety certification.

Can you fit an indoor play area into an existing mall or clubhouse?

Yes. Structures are made in modules in Korea and assembled on site, so they can go into existing buildings. We check access routes, ceiling heights and floor loading during the survey.
