What is a kids play area?

A kids play area is a dedicated, fenced or zoned space with playground equipment such as play towers, slides, swings and climbing nets, set on impact-absorbing safety surfacing. In Indian housing societies and schools it usually includes separate zones for toddlers and for children aged 5–12.

FAQ

Kids play area questions

What equipment goes into a kids play area for a housing society?

A typical society play area combines one combination play structure (towers, slides, climbing), a separate toddler zone, freestanding swings or spinners, and impact-absorbing safety surfacing. Larger societies add a sculptural landmark piece, an in-ground trampoline or a water play feature.

How much space does a kids play area need?

Each piece of equipment needs its own footprint plus a clear safety zone around it, typically at least 1.5 to 2 metres on every side. Even a compact area can hold a small combination unit and a toddler zone; a full society play area usually needs several hundred square metres. We work out the exact layout from your site plan.

What does a kids play area cost in India?

Cost depends on the size of the area, the design (a complete play area or a sculptural landmark piece), the amount of safety surfacing, shade structures and site work. We give a detailed quote once we have designed the area for your space.

Which safety standards does your playground equipment meet?

Our play equipment carries EN 1176 and ASTM international safety certifications and Korea's KC certification for children's products. Every installation is planned with safety zones and impact-absorbing surfacing matched to the critical fall height of each piece.

Will the equipment survive Indian summers and monsoons?

The equipment is built for permanent outdoor use and long service life. During design we also plan shade, drainage and surfacing for local conditions, which matters as much as the equipment itself in Indian summers and monsoons.

Do you install the play area and provide a warranty?

Yes. We handle import, installation and handover on site, and provide the manufacturer's warranty and after-sales support in India. Warranty terms are set out in your quote.
