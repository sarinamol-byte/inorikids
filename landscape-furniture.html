FAQ

Landscape furniture questions

What is landscape furniture?

Landscape furniture, also called site furniture or street furniture, is the fixed outdoor furniture in a landscape: benches, pergolas, pavilions, shelters, planters, bollards and public art. It makes outdoor spaces comfortable and gives them a recognisable identity.

What is the difference between a pergola and a pavilion?

A pergola is an open frame of posts and beams, sometimes with slats or a light canopy, that gives partial shade and defines a walkway or seating area. A pavilion is a fully roofed structure, sometimes enclosed with glass, that gives full shelter and can be used as a lounge, tea house or meeting space.

Can pergolas and pavilions be customised?

Yes. Every pavilion and pergola is designed for your landscape plan, and our team also creates one-off pavilions and canopies around your brand.

Which materials suit Indian weather?

For strong sun and heavy rain we recommend powder-coated steel or aluminium frames, stainless fixings, treated or composite timber and UV-stable finishes. Our partners powder-coat in-house, which keeps finish quality consistent.

Do you supply landscape furniture for podium gardens and terraces?

Yes. We check loading, fixing details and wind exposure for podiums and terraces, and choose bases and anchoring to suit the slab.
