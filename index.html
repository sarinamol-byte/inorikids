In short

Inori Kids is an Indian company, partnered with Chungwoo Funstation of South Korea, bringing world-class kids play areas, landscape and recreational solutions from Korea to India. Korea is home to some of the world's most inventive play and landscape design, and through the Chungwoo Funstation group (with its sister companies Spacetalk and Ittang) we bring the best of those designs and materials to Indian projects. We supply and install kids play areas, indoor play areas, landscape furniture, kids furniture and outdoor gym equipment for residential developers, contractors, schools, malls and hotels across India. You give us the space; we design the solution around it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Inori Kids do?

Inori Kids is an Indian company, partnered with Chungwoo Funstation of South Korea, bringing world-class kids play areas, landscape and recreational solutions from Korea to India. We design, import, install and guarantee outdoor play areas, indoor play, landscape furniture (pergolas, pavilions, benches), kids furniture and outdoor gyms for developers, contractors, schools, malls and hotels.

Who are your clients?

Residential developers and housing societies, schools and preschools, malls and retail centres, and hotels and resorts. We also work with landscape architects and contractors on their projects.

How does a project with Inori Kids start?

You give us the area, your site plan and what you want there. Developers and contractors often come with a space and a product in mind and ask us to design the area around it. Our award-winning design team then designs the complete play area, landscape or open gym for that space, and we import, install and guarantee it.

Do you sell off-the-shelf play equipment?

No. We are a kids play area solution provider: every project is designed for the client's space, from the layout and safety zones to the equipment, surfacing and shade, and can include a one-of-a-kind landmark piece.

Where is the equipment made?

In South Korea, in the factories of our partner Chungwoo Funstation and its sister companies, with in-house manufacturing and powder coating. We handle import, installation, warranty and after-sales in India.

Is the equipment safety certified?

Our play equipment carries EN 1176 and ASTM international safety certifications and Korea's KC certification for children's products. Every installation is planned with safety zones and impact-absorbing surfacing matched to the critical fall height of each piece.

Which cities do you work in?

We are based in Gurugram, Haryana, and take on projects across India, including Delhi NCR (Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad, Ghaziabad), Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Jaipur and Lucknow.
