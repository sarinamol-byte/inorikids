FAQ

Bespoke design questions

What is a bespoke or custom playground?

A bespoke playground is play equipment designed from scratch for one site. It is usually built around a theme, such as an animal, a local story or a brand, and becomes a landmark for the project.

How does the bespoke design process work?

We start with your site plan, users and design intent. The partners' designers develop a concept and 3D design, engineer it for safety, manufacture it in their own factory in Korea, and we install it on site in India.

Is a custom play structure safe?

Yes. Bespoke pieces are engineered to EN 1176 safety principles, with safety zones, fall heights and surfacing planned in the design.

Can a bespoke design reflect our project's brand or theme?

Yes. That is the point of bespoke work. We are currently developing custom concepts for residential developers in India, each built around the developer's design intent.
