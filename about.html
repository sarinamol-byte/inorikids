About Inori Kids

An Indian company bringing Korea's best play spaces to India

Inori Kids is an Indian company, partnered with Chungwoo Funstation of South Korea, bringing world-class kids play areas, landscape and recreational solutions from Korea to India. Korea is home to some of the world's most inventive play and landscape design, and through the Chungwoo Funstation group (with its sister companies Spacetalk and Ittang) we bring the best of those designs and materials to Indian projects. We design with our partners, import, install on site and provide the guarantee in India.

Talk to usSee projects

What we do

Developers, schools, malls and hotels deal with one accountable team in India, from the first sketch to handover: design, import, installation, safety surfacing, warranty and after-sales.

We are based in Gurugram, Haryana, and take on projects across India. Our first projects are bespoke concepts for residential developers, each designed around the developer's design intent.

Why Korea

From Korea's best play designers, to Indian projects.

Korean developers have made designed play areas and landscapes the centrepiece of residential and public projects for decades. We bring that know-how to India.

Korean design leadership

Award-winning play, landscape and furniture design, recognised by the Red Dot, iF and Good Design awards and Korea's Presidential Award.

Our partner since day one

Chungwoo Funstation has designed and built play spaces since 1988, with its own factories and sister companies Spacetalk (landscape) and Ittang (indoor play and kids furniture).

An Indian company

Inori Kids is based in Gurugram. We adapt every design to Indian sites, climate and users, and handle import, installation and warranty here.

One team for your project

Developers and contractors deal with one accountable Indian team, from the first sketch to handover and after-sales.
